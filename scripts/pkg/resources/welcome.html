Vaultwrap

An encrypted vault for your .env secrets. It injects them into your apps and terminals, and connects to Azure Key Vault, .env files or any CLI.

This installs:

  • Vaultwrap.app in Applications. It lives in your menu bar.
  • The vaultwrap command at /usr/local/bin/vaultwrap
  • A background service that starts the vault daemon when you log in
